# Data Analytics

I use Python to clean data, examine distributions, compare business and operational variables, and communicate patterns through visualisations.

## Automobile pricing and exploratory analysis

I explored vehicle prices, specifications, fuel efficiency and body styles to understand how different automobile attributes relate to price. I cleaned the records, converted numerical fields, examined distributions, and compared relationships through charts and correlations.

My analysis is in [automobile.ipynb](automobile.ipynb), with dependencies in [requirements.txt](requirements.txt). To reproduce it, place a compatible `automobile.txt` dataset alongside the notebook.

The notebook is an exploratory analysis; it does not train a car-price prediction model.
